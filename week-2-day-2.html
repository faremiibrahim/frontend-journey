OUR GOAL

id_casual is an online fashion destination created to make everyday style simple, accessible, and inclusive. We offer a carefully curated range of men’s and women’s clothing, along with fashion accessories designed to complement different lifestyles and personal styles.
OUR OBJECTIVE

Our goal is to provide quality casual wear that blends comfort with modern trends. At id_casual, we believe fashion should be effortless — pieces that can be worn confidently for daily activities, social outings, and special moments without compromising comfort or individuality.
OUR COMMITMENT

We are committed to growing into a trusted fashion brand that values style, simplicity, and customer satisfaction. As we continue to expand our collections and improve our platform, id_casual remains dedicated to helping customers express themselves through fashion that feels good and looks even better.

As part of my frontend learning journey, I’m currently building a website called id_casual. It’s an online fashion store that focuses on both men’s and women’s wear, alongside a wide range of fashion accessories. The idea behind id_casual is simple: create a clean, modern, and easy-to-navigate online shop where casual fashion meets everyday style. This project gives me the opportunity to practice real-world frontend skills while working on something practical and relatable. At this stage of development, I’m focusing on the layout and structure of the website. I’ve started building components like the navigation bar and product card layouts using HTML and CSS, especially Flexbox. This helps me understand how professional e-commerce websites arrange items, manage spacing, and stay responsive across different screen sizes. Beyond just writing code, id_casual is helping me think more like a product builder — considering user experience, clarity, and simplicity. Each section I build improves my confidence and pushes me to apply what I learn immediately. As I progress, I plan to expand the project by adding more pages, improving responsiveness, and eventually introducing interactivity. id_casual is not just a practice project; it’s a reflection of my growth as a frontend developer.
